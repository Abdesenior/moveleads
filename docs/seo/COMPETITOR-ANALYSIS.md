# Competitor Analysis

_From live Google results on 2026-09-29. No paid SEO tool was used, so domain authority and traffic figures are not included. Connect DataForSEO or Ahrefs later for numbers._

Free link and traffic |

**Takeaways**

- The B2B SERP has no giant brands. Exact-match, well-structured pages with clear pricing rank here.
- Price in the title is common and clearly works. Our "$10" starting price is a real advantage.
- Nobody explains quality checks in detail. A page showing our Twilio check, scoring and refund policy stands out.

## B2C: "free moving quotes"

Owned by van lines and big publishers: [Allied](https://www.allied.com/free-moving-quotes), [North American](https://www.northamerican.com/free-moving-quote), [moveBuddha](https://www.movebuddha.com/moving-cost-calculator/), [iMoving](https://www.imoving.com/moving-cost-calculator/), [Movers.com](https://www.movers.com/), [U.S. News](https://realestate.usnews.com/home-services/moving/moving-quotes), [Sirelo](https://sirelo.com/moving-quotes/), [Unpakt](https://www.unpakt.com/). **Do not target this head term in year one.**

## B2C: "moving from Chicago to Dallas" (route long tail)

Ranking: [moveBuddha](https://www.movebuddha.com/popular-routes/il/tx/chicago-to-dallas/), [Allied](https://www.allied.com/moving-companies/illinois/moving-to/chicago-to-dallas), [iMoving](https://www.imoving.com/blog/moving-tips/moving-from-chicago-to-dallas/), [MoveAdvisor](https://moveadvisor.com/usa/routes/chicago-to-dallas-movers), [myGoodMovers](https://mygoodmovers.com/popular-routes/il/chicago-to-dallas), [MoverQuoteEngine](https://www.moverquoteengine.com/moving-from-chicago-us-to-dallas-us), plus local movers ([Alliance](https://www.alliancemoving.com/long-distance-moving-company/chicago-dallas-movers), [Born To Move](https://www.goborntomove.com/movers-from-chicago-to-dallas), [STI](https://www.stimovers.com/dallas-to-chicago/)).

**Takeaways**

- Every winner puts a **price range in the title**: "($812+) Chicago to Dallas Movers", "$3,010 - $8,600 Movers from Chicago to Dallas for 2026".
- Smaller sites (myGoodMovers, MoverQuoteEngine) rank here, which proves a young domain can win route pages.
- Winning pages show: distance, transit days, costs by service type and home size, and the city-to-city comparison.
- This is our best B2C opportunity, and our pricing engine already produces the numbers.
