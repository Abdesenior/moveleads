## Rules for every piece

- First paragraph answers the query directly, in 2 to 3 sentences (for AI citation).
- An FAQ block with 4 to 6 real questions, marked up as FAQPage.
- Numbers are sourced and linked, or come from our own data with the method stated.
- Author byline with a real name and role (E-E-A-T). The founder is the author for B2B pieces.
- One primary call to action. B2B: "Create a free mover account". B2C: "Get free quotes".
