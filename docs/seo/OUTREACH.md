# Outreach kit: get MoveLeads listed and linked

Goal: be added to the "best moving lead providers" articles that already rank on page 1, and earn links. Send from your own email. Find each contact through the site's contact page, the article author's byline, or LinkedIn. Do not buy links or offer payment for placement; that breaks Google's spam policies.

Track every send in the table at the bottom. Follow up once after 5 to 7 days, then stop.
